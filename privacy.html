Budgy Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Budgy ("the app") is a personal budget and spending tracker made by Omar Mokhtar ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

The short version

  • Without an account, everything stays on your device. We never see it.
  • If you choose to sign in, your Budgy data is stored in your account as a backup, so you can get it back if you change phones or reinstall the app. It is used for nothing else.
  • If you turn on SMS import (Android only), your bank texts are read on your phone only. The messages themselves never leave it.
  • If you buy Budgy Pro, the store handles the payment and RevenueCat keeps the record of your purchase. We never see your card or payment details.
  • If you turn on Lock Budgy (Android only), the app asks for your phone's own unlock: your fingerprint, face, or the phone's PIN, pattern or password. Your phone checks it; Budgy never sees or stores it.
  • No ads, no tracking, and your data is never sold.
  • You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app at any time.

Data the app handles

Your budget data: accounts, categories, transactions, notes, budgets, currencies and exchange rates, and your profile name and colour.

  • Not signed in: this is stored only on your device. Backups you make are files you control.
  • Signed in: it is also stored in our cloud database as a backup, so you can get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling.

Account information, only if you sign in: your email address and a user ID from Google or Apple, and your name if Apple shares it on first sign-in. These identify your account and are used for nothing else. With Apple's "Hide My Email", we only see a relay address.

Bank text messages, only if you turn on SMS import (Android): Budgy can read your bank's text messages and turn them into transactions for you to review. It's off until you turn it on in Bank texts, and Android asks for your permission first.

  • Budgy reads only texts from the bank senders you list, and only on your phone. It reads them when you tap "Scan inbox", or, if you turn on "Scan when the app opens", each time you open Budgy (looking only at texts that arrived since the last scan). It doesn't read texts in the background or when a text arrives.
  • While a text waits for your review, its message is kept on your phone. It's deleted as soon as you confirm or dismiss it. Message text is never uploaded, backed up or shared.
  • A text you confirm becomes an ordinary transaction: its amount, date and merchant. To suggest the right account and category next time, Budgy remembers which bank sender and last card or account digits belong to which of your accounts, and which category you chose for each merchant.
  • Signed in: those learned card digits and merchant names are stored in your account with the rest of your Budgy data, so your other phones can use them. Each imported transaction also carries a code so the same text is never added twice. The code is made from the sender and the time the text arrived, not from what it says.
  • You can review and remove learned card numbers in Bank texts → settings → Linked numbers. Turning SMS import off stops Budgy reading texts.

Budgy Pro purchases, only if you buy or restore Budgy Pro: Budgy Pro (monthly, yearly or lifetime) unlocks bank texts and sign-in with sync. It's sold through Google Play or the App Store, which take the payment under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see your card, bank or payment details.

  • To know whether you have Budgy Pro, the app uses RevenueCat (revenuecat.com), a subscription service. RevenueCat receives the store's receipt for your purchase (which product, when it was bought, renews or ends, and whether it's a trial) and an ID for you: a random ID made on your phone, or, once you sign in, your Budgy account ID, so your Pro follows your account.
  • This is used to unlock Budgy Pro on your devices, to help you if a purchase doesn't show up (the "Support ID" on your Profile is this ID), and for purchase analytics: RevenueCat's reports show us figures such as how many people start a trial, subscribe, renew or cancel. It isn't used for ads or tracking.
  • Even if you never buy it, the app asks RevenueCat whether you have Budgy Pro, so RevenueCat receives that random ID and basic technical details (app version, platform, language). It receives no budget data.

Things we don't collect: location, contacts, photos, advertising IDs, or usage analytics (what you do in the app).

Where the data is stored and who processes it

  • Supabase (supabase.com) hosts our database and sign-in service, in the EU (Ireland) region. Supabase processes the data only to provide this service.
  • Google and Apple handle the sign-in itself when you choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Apple", under their own privacy policies. Google Play and the App Store handle Budgy Pro payments.
  • RevenueCat keeps the record of Budgy Pro purchases described above, and processes it only to provide this service, including the purchase reports it shows us.
  • The data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). In the database, access rules limit each account's data to that account.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Not signed in: data stays until you delete it in the app (Menu → Data → Erase all data) or uninstall the app.
  • Signed in: cloud data is kept while your account exists.
  • Profile → Account & sync → Delete account permanently deletes your account and all its cloud data. You can choose whether to keep a copy on the device.
  • Signing out leaves your data in your account. You choose whether to keep a copy on the device.
  • Budgy Pro purchase records are kept by the store and RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide and account for the purchase. Deleting your Budgy account doesn't cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play or the App Store. To have your RevenueCat record deleted, email us with your Support ID.
  • Can't open the app? See Delete your Budgy account.

Children

Budgy is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
