Budgy Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Budgy ("the app") is a personal budget and spending tracker made by Omar Mokhtar ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

The short version

  • Without an account, everything stays on your device. We never see it.
  • If you choose to sign in, your Budgy data is stored in your account as a backup, so you can get it back if you change phones or reinstall the app. It is used for nothing else.
  • If you turn on SMS import (Android only), your bank texts are read on your phone only. The messages themselves never leave it.
  • No ads, no analytics, no selling or sharing of data.
  • You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app at any time.

Data the app handles

Your budget data: accounts, categories, transactions, notes, budgets, currencies and exchange rates, and your profile name and colour.

  • Not signed in: this is stored only on your device. Backups you make are files you control.
  • Signed in: it is also stored in our cloud database as a backup, so you can get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling.

Account information, only if you sign in: your email address and a user ID from Google or Apple, and your name if Apple shares it on first sign-in. These identify your account and are used for nothing else. With Apple's "Hide My Email", we only see a relay address.

Bank text messages, only if you turn on SMS import (Android): Budgy can read your bank's text messages and turn them into transactions for you to review. It's off until you turn it on in Settings → Import from SMS, and Android asks for your permission first.

  • Budgy reads only texts from the bank senders you list, only on your phone, and only when you tap "Scan inbox". It doesn't read texts in the background.
  • While a text waits for your review, its message is kept on your phone. It's deleted as soon as you confirm or dismiss it. Message text is never uploaded, backed up or shared.
  • A text you confirm becomes an ordinary transaction: its amount, date and merchant. To suggest the right account and category next time, Budgy remembers which bank sender and last card or account digits belong to which of your accounts, and which category you chose for each merchant.
  • Signed in: those learned card digits and merchant names are stored in your account with the rest of your Budgy data, so your other phones can use them. Each imported transaction also carries a code so the same text is never added twice. The code is made from the sender and the time the text arrived, not from what it says.
  • You can review and remove learned card numbers in Settings → Import from SMS → Linked numbers. Turning SMS import off stops Budgy reading texts.

Things we don't collect: location, contacts, photos, advertising IDs, or usage analytics.

Where the data is stored and who processes it

  • Supabase (supabase.com) hosts our database and sign-in service, in the EU (Ireland) region. Supabase processes the data only to provide this service.
  • Google and Apple handle the sign-in itself when you choose "Continue with Google" or "Continue with Apple", under their own privacy policies.
  • The data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). In the database, access rules limit each account's data to that account.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Not signed in: data stays until you delete it in the app (Menu → Data → Erase all data) or uninstall the app.
  • Signed in: cloud data is kept while your account exists.
  • Profile → Account & sync → Delete account permanently deletes your account and all its cloud data. You can choose whether to keep a copy on the device.
  • Signing out leaves your data in your account. You choose whether to keep a copy on the device.
  • Can't open the app? See Delete your Budgy account.

Children

Budgy is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.

Contact
