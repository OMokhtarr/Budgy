Delete your Budgy account

Last updated: November 4, 2026

This page explains how to delete your Budgy account and the data tied to it. You only have a Budgy account if you signed in with Google or Apple. If you never signed in, there is no account to delete: your data is only on your device. To remove it, use Menu → Data → Erase all data, or uninstall the app.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Budgy and go to Menu → Profile.
  2. Under Account & sync, tap Delete account.
  3. Read "Delete your account?" and choose Delete, keep data on this device or Delete everything.
  4. Budgy shows "Account deleted" and signs you out.

If deletion fails (for example, with no connection), the app says so, your account stays, and you can try again when you are online.

Or ask us by email

If you cannot open the app, email or.mokhtar@gmail.com from the email address on your Budgy account, with the subject "Delete my account". Mention whether you signed in with Google or Apple. We will confirm that the request comes from the account holder and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. It removes:

  • Your Budgy account: your user ID, email address, name, and sign-in details.
  • Everything stored in your account: the accounts, categories, transactions, notes, budgets, and settings synced to it.

What is not deleted

  • Data on this device, if you choose Delete, keep data on this device.
  • Copies on your other devices. They stay there until you use Erase all data or uninstall the app on each one.
  • Backup files you made. These are your own files, so delete them yourself.
  • Budgy's link to your Apple ID, if you signed in with Apple. Remove Budgy yourself in your Apple ID settings → Sign in with Apple.

See also the Privacy Policy.
