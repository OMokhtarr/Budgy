## Bank texts (Android)

Bank texts is optional and off until you turn it on and allow Android's SMS permission. Budgy then reads only
texts from the bank senders you list, on your phone. It suggests a transaction for each text, which may be wrong
or incomplete: review it before you save it. Only use bank texts on a phone whose messages you're entitled to
read.
